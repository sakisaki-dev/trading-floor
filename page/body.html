Governmentthe switch in the bar · a scripted government in this browser

The page can't run a Claude session, so the governments here are the scripted ones of gov/arms.mjs and the stub. The real government is a Claude session that regulate.mjs runs on your Mac (your subscription; nothing here starts one): node regulate.mjs --government claude --brief v1 --model claude-haiku-5-5 --session-root /Users/Shared/tf-gov/g1 --seed 1 --weeks 7000 --pace 4 --out runs/g1 --serve 4322, then open http://localhost:4322. See docs/GOVERNMENT.md.
